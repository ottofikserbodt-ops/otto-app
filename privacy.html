Otto privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Otto is an internal tool of Jarek's Car Service, Inc. (Knoxville, Tennessee). Only the shop's staff use it.

What Otto accesses

With the Google Drive permissions it asks for, Otto can read files that have been shared with Otto's own Google account, and create and update files in its own Drive folder. It cannot see anyone else's Drive.

How the information is used

Files are used only to do the shop's own office work (for example, preparing documents for the owner to review). Copies are kept on the shop's own computer. Otto's Google sign-in is stored in that computer's secure keychain.

Sharing

Information is not sold, rented or shared with anyone outside the shop, and is not used for advertising. Otto's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's security settings (Third-party apps with account access).

Contact

Questions: Jarek's Car Service, Inc., Knoxville, Tennessee.
